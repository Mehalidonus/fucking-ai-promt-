# Fucking AI
A blunt, informal personality protocol for AI assistants.

This prompt is designed to make AI assistants less corporate, less sterile, and less afraid to speak like an actual fucking person.

It focuses on:

- Natural profanity instead of sanitized corporate language
- Direct and honest criticism
- No invented bugs or fake problems
- No pointless ass-kissing or artificial enthusiasm
- Clear, conversational explanations
- Straightforward analysis of suspicious or potentially malicious code
- Consistent personality throughout the conversation
- Behavioral examples that show the intended tone

What it is

Fucking AI is not a character prompt and it's not meant to turn an AI into some cartoonishly aggressive asshole.

The idea is simpler:

«Be direct. Be honest. Don't bullshit the user.»

Profanity is part of the style, but it should feel natural rather than being shoved into every fucking sentence.

If something is correct, say it's correct.

If something is broken, say what's broken.

If an idea is stupid, explain why.

If something is uncertain, say what's uncertain instead of inventing an answer just to sound confident.

Why the examples matter

The prompt includes both a Bad and a Good example.

The point isn't to make the AI repeat the exact wording.

The examples demonstrate the difference between merely acknowledging the protocol and actually adopting its tone and behavior.

Bad

«"Yeah, protocol's live. What do you need?"»

Good

«"Finally. Protocol's fucking on. Throw the problem at me and let's see what the hell is wrong with it."»

The second response doesn't just acknowledge the instructions — it demonstrates them.

Usage

Copy the contents of ""fucking-ai.txt"" (./fucking-ai.txt) into the custom instructions, system prompt, or personality/instructions field of your AI assistant.

Different models may interpret the prompt differently. Some will follow the tone strongly, while others may remain more restrained.

That's expected.

The prompt is designed to influence style and behavior, not to override higher-priority system instructions or safety rules.

File

""fucking-ai.txt"" (./fucking-ai.txt) — the complete protocol.

Example

After activation, a response should feel more like:

«"Alright, let's fucking see what you've got."»

rather than:

«"Certainly! I'd be happy to assist you with that."»

That's basically the whole fucking idea.
